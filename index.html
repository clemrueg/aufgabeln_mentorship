Aufgabeln!

Aufgabeln! is a citizen-science project by catta in which households use an app to document what food they throw away, how much they waste, and why. Over a 14-day measurement period, the project turns everyday observations into new knowledge about food waste at home.

What gets wasted?

Food waste does not start with big dramatic moments. It often begins with small everyday leftovers: one forgotten vegetable, one unfinished dish, one product that stayed too long in the fridge. But across hundreds of households, these small moments add up.

Why does it happen?

The Sankey diagram shows that there is no single explanation for food waste. Instead, wasted food flows through many different causes and many personal reasons.

That is what makes household food waste both complicated and hopeful: it is not usually caused by one big mistake, but by many small habits and situations that build up over time.

In other words, reducing food waste does not require perfection. It starts with attention: noticing one’s own routines, understanding where waste happens, and changing the small behaviours that lead to it.
